Rule #1 Full Story

Technical Summary

Purpose:

The Full Story is the highest conviction document in the Rule One process.

It integrates:
	•	Event analysis
	•	Meaning
	•	Moat
	•	Management
	•	Valuation confirmation
	•	Inversion & rebuttal
	•	Trading strategy
	•	Investment strategy

It answers one final question:

Would I confidently own this entire business for life?

This document converts the template into a structured analytical system.

⸻

1. Event Analysis

Objective:

Determine whether a price drop represents opportunity or structural failure.

Required:
	•	Describe event and magnitude of drop
	•	Timeline of event
	•	Expected resolution window
	•	Debt implications
	•	Prior similar events
	•	Analyst reactions

Key Questions:
	•	Will it resolve within 3 years?
	•	Will it require new debt?
	•	Is it temporary or thesis-breaking?

Conclusion must confirm:
You have read all relevant articles and analyst reports.

⸻

2. Meaning Analysis

Radar
	•	How did it get on your radar?
	•	Is it owned by Gurus (>4% position)?
	•	What is your informational edge?

⸻

Business Understanding

Must clearly describe:
	•	Product/service
	•	Revenue sources
	•	Business segments
	•	Profit concentration
	•	Industry structure
	•	Cycle (if cyclical)
	•	Problem solved
	•	Dominant positioning

Must be able to:
	•	Explain KPIs
	•	Predict 10-year industry strength
	•	Articulate competitive advantage
	•	Align with personal values

If unclear → reject.

⸻

3. Moat Analysis

Must understand:
	•	Competitive advantage type
	•	Brand
	•	Switching
	•	Network
	•	Toll
	•	Secrets
	•	Barriers to entry
	•	Intrinsic defensibility
	•	Price raising power
	•	Recurring revenue
	•	Customer loyalty
	•	Supplier relationships
	•	Market share durability

Questions:
	•	Could you replicate this business with its market cap?
	•	What happens if it disappears?
	•	Has moat strengthened or weakened?
	•	Is it anti-fragile?

Conclusion:
Still confident business will be bigger in 10 years.

⸻

4. Management Analysis

Leadership Evaluation
	•	CEO experience
	•	Track record
	•	Capital allocation discipline
	•	Insider ownership
	•	Stock accumulation behavior
	•	Compensation alignment
	•	Reputation

⸻

Financial Discipline
	•	ROE high and stable?
	•	ROIC high and stable?
	•	ROA high and stable?
	•	Debt improving?
	•	FCF ≥ 75% of earnings?
	•	Owner Earnings ≥ 75% of earnings?
	•	Maintenance CapEx low?
	•	Moat independent of CEO personality?

Conclusion:
Trust long-term stewardship.

⸻

5. Valuation Confirmation

Must justify:
	•	Industry strength in 10 years
	•	Company strength in 10 years
	•	Historical growth consistency (≥7 years)
	•	Organic vs acquisition growth
	•	Debt-fueled growth risk

⸻

Buy Prices

MOS
	•	Growth rate
	•	EPS basis
	•	Future P/E
	•	MARR (15%)
	•	Sticker price
	•	MOS price

PBT
	•	FCF ratio
	•	FCF per share
	•	Years to payback

Ten Cap
	•	Cash from operations
	•	CapEx
	•	Maintenance %
	•	Tax provision
	•	Owner earnings
	•	Shares outstanding

Conclusion:
Confident valuation estimate is reasonable.

⸻

6. Inversion & Rebuttal

Identify Failure Scenarios
	•	Structural industry shifts
	•	Debt risks
	•	Leadership risk
	•	Disruption
	•	Legal or regulatory catastrophe

⸻

Inversion Protocol

For every reason to own:

Create inversion.
Create rebuttal.
Validate rebuttal.

If rebuttal weak → reduce size or avoid.

Must also:
	•	Review short-seller theses
	•	Review analyst hold/sell ratings

Conclusion:
All inversions addressed.

⸻

7. Initial Trading Strategy

Before full investment:
	•	Backtest valuation methods
	•	Determine lifecycle stage
	•	Identify best-performing valuation lens
	•	Define trade size limits
	•	Define assignment plan
	•	Set price alerts

Conclusion:
Trading plan exists before execution.

⸻

8. Investment Strategy

Define:
	•	Portfolio allocation %
	•	Tranche structure
	•	Entry method (ROP, equity, ROCT)
	•	Exit strategy
	•	PACE Plan:
	•	Primary
	•	Alternative
	•	Contingency
	•	Emergency

Conclusion:
Full lifecycle plan defined.

⸻

Structural Insight

The Full Story is:

Not screening.
Not research.
Not valuation.

It is conviction engineering.

You cannot complete this document without:
	•	Understanding the business deeply
	•	Stress testing assumptions
	•	Defining capital allocation discipline
	•	Removing emotion

It is the final gate before meaningful capital deployment.
