Rule One Story Form Resources

Technical Summary

Purpose:
Provide practical tools and data sources for completing the Story Form.

Organized by:
	•	Radar
	•	Meaning
	•	Management
	•	Valuation

This document converts the resource list into a structured research workflow.

⸻

1. Radar — Guru Identification

Objective:

Determine whether respected investors (“Gurus”) own the company.

Process:
	•	Use Rule One Toolbox → Search → Search Guru Stocks
	•	Click the company symbol
	•	Review Guru ownership

Interpretation:
	•	Current ownership
	•	Position size significance
	•	Recent additions or exits
	•	Historical conviction patterns

Guru ownership provides:
Context, not confirmation.

⸻

2. Meaning — Understanding the Business

Step 1: Read the Annual Report

Open:
	•	10K (US)
	•	20F / 40F (International)

Focus on:
	•	Business section
	•	Competitive positioning
	•	Risk disclosures

Ask:
	•	Can I understand this business?
	•	Does it match my values?
	•	Does it have a strong moat?

Moat types:
	•	Brand
	•	Price advantage
	•	Secrets
	•	Switching costs
	•	Toll bridge

If it fails clarity → reject.

⸻

3. Management — Leadership Evaluation

Investor Relations Review
	•	Locate Shareholder Annual Report
	•	Read CEO letter

Look for:
	•	Long-term vision
	•	Capital allocation discipline
	•	Transparency

⸻

Earnings Call Analysis

Use Seeking Alpha:
	•	Read Management remarks
	•	Read Q&A section

Evaluate:
	•	Tone consistency
	•	Defensive responses
	•	Strategy clarity
	•	Accountability

⸻

CEO Background Research

Google:
	•	Career history
	•	Past successes/failures
	•	Passion for business
	•	Big Audacious Goal (BAG)

Questions:
	•	Is this leader mission-driven?
	•	Do incentives align with shareholders?

⸻

4. Valuation — MOS Protocol

Margin of Safety (MOS)

Steps:
	1.	Verify EPS TTM against multiple sources.
	2.	Confirm consistency with historical earnings.
	3.	Determine conservative Future Growth Rate (FGR).
	4.	Compare FGR to Toolbox default and use the lower.
	5.	Adjust Future P/E:
	•	≈ 2 × Growth Rate
	•	Not above historical high P/E

Conservatism required.

⸻

5. 8-Year Payback Time

Use GuruFocus:
	•	Review 30-year financials (or 5-year free version)
	•	Verify FCF per share (TTM)
	•	Confirm consistency across years

Purpose:
Validate free cash flow durability.

⸻

Structural Workflow Summary

Story Form Research Flow:

Radar → Meaning → Management → Valuation

Each stage builds conviction incrementally.

Never skip forward.

⸻

Core Principles
	•	Always verify numbers from multiple sources.
	•	Always prefer conservative assumptions.
	•	Always test understanding before valuation.
	•	Always validate growth assumptions against industry reality.

Story Form Resources are not just links.

They are a research discipline system:

Understand → Validate → Stress Test → Price Rationally

Conviction must be earned.
