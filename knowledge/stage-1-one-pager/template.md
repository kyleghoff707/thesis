Rule #1 One Pager

Technical Summary

Purpose:
The One Pager is a high-level screening tool used before building a full Pitch Deck.

It answers one core question:

Is this company worth deeper research?

It combines:
	•	Basic company information
	•	Minimum financial standards
	•	Meaning KPIs
	•	Management KPIs
	•	Growth snapshot

The One Pager is a filter — not a conviction document.

⸻

1. Company Information

Collect:
	•	Company Name
	•	Ticker
	•	Last Price
	•	Industry
	•	Market Cap (in millions)
	•	Headquarters

Purpose:
Establish scale, sector, and context.

⸻

2. Minimum Standards

Initial financial filters:
	•	Public ≥ 7 years?
	•	Debt < 3 years earnings?
	•	Market Cap threshold acceptable?
	•	Bigger in 10 years? (Qualitative)

These eliminate structurally weak companies quickly.

⸻

3. Meaning KPIs

Evaluate whether the company:
	•	Is within your “3 Circles” (Passion, Talent, Money)
	•	Has Guru ownership
	•	Has strong Rule One Score
	•	Appears durable long-term

This section checks:

Understandability + Personal alignment + External validation.

⸻

4. Management KPIs

Key metrics:
	•	Return on Equity (ROE)
	•	Return on Invested Capital (ROIC)
	•	Net Debt to Earnings
	•	Net Debt to FCF

Purpose:
Quick capital allocation check.

High returns + low debt = preliminary green flag.

⸻

5. Growth Metrics

Insert:
	•	Revenue growth
	•	EPS growth
	•	FCF growth
	•	Equity growth

Focus on:
	•	10-year consistency
	•	Double-digit compounding
	•	Predictability

If growth is inconsistent or volatile:
Proceed cautiously.

⸻

6. Company Summary

Short narrative:
	•	What they do
	•	How they make money
	•	Why they might be durable
	•	Why they might not be

This is a clarity test.

If you cannot summarize clearly:
It fails “Simple.”

⸻

Screening Logic

A company passes the One Pager if:
	•	It meets minimum standards
	•	Returns are strong
	•	Debt is manageable
	•	Growth is consistent
	•	Business is understandable
	•	It fits within your circles

If it passes:
→ Build the Pitch Deck.

If not:
→ Discard and move on.

⸻

Structural Insight

The One Pager is:

Filter → Not conviction
Snapshot → Not thesis
Screen → Not valuation

It prevents wasted research time.
