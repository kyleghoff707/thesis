Tools for Analyzing the Three Ms (Moat, Management, MOS)

Technical Summary

Purpose:
Provide a structured research protocol for evaluating:
	•	Moat
	•	Management
	•	Margin of Safety (MOS)

This document converts the original checklist into a reasoning framework for disciplined investment analysis.

⸻

1. Moat Analysis Framework

Step 1: Infer Moat from Numbers

If long-term metrics are strong:
	•	Revenue growth
	•	Earnings growth
	•	Strong ROIC/ROE
	•	Consistent FCF

Then a moat likely exists.

Your task:
Define it explicitly.

⸻

Step 2: Peer Comparison

Use competitor comparison to determine:
	•	Best-of-breed positioning
	•	Margin superiority
	•	Return metric dominance
	•	Market share trajectory

Key Question:
Why are they better than competitors?

⸻

Step 3: Primary Research Sources

SEC Filings
	•	10K → “Business” section
	•	Risk factors
	•	Competitive positioning

Investor Relations
	•	Earnings call transcripts
	•	CEO letters
	•	Investor presentations

External Tools
	•	Seeking Alpha
	•	GuruFocus
	•	Analyst reports
	•	Yahoo Finance competitors
	•	Industry trade journals

⸻

Moat Questions
	•	Do they have a moat?
	•	What type? (Brand, Price, Secrets, Switching, Toll Bridge)
	•	Is it durable for 20+ years?
	•	Has it strengthened or weakened?
	•	Why can’t competitors replicate it?

⸻

2. Management Evaluation Framework

Evaluate whether management is:
	•	Experienced
	•	Honest
	•	Passionate
	•	Shareholder-oriented

⸻

Management Metrics
	•	ROE / ROIC / ROA trends
	•	Debt discipline
	•	Insider ownership
	•	Capital allocation history
	•	Earnings call tone consistency

⸻

Key Questions
	•	Is growth organic or debt-fueled?
	•	Are buybacks done below intrinsic value?
	•	Is compensation aligned with performance?
	•	Do insiders own meaningful stock?

⸻

3. Margin of Safety (MOS) Framework

Valuation Outputs:
	•	Future Growth Rate (FGR)
	•	Sticker Price
	•	MOS Price (50% of Sticker)
	•	Payback Time Price
	•	Tangible Book Value

⸻

MOS Questions
	•	Is FGR realistic and conservative?
	•	Is valuation supported by history?
	•	Is growth sustainable?
	•	Does price provide a sufficient discount?

⸻

Final Investment Question

If this qualifies as a Wonderful Rule #1 “Just One” company:

At what price would I confidently buy it today?

Only deploy capital when:

Moat + Management + MOS align.
