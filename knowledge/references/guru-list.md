# Guru Reference List

## Purpose

This document defines the term **"Guru"** as used throughout the Rule
One Investing materials.

Whenever the term **Guru** is referenced in any Rule One slides, Pitch
Decks, Story Forms, or research frameworks, it refers to:

> Respected professional investors who actively manage capital through
> registered investment funds and whose holdings are publicly disclosed
> through SEC filings (Form 13F).

These investors typically manage hedge funds, mutual funds, or
investment partnerships and are considered experienced capital
allocators with publicly trackable portfolios.

Claude should use this document as the authoritative reference list
whenever the term **Guru** is mentioned.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## What Makes Someone a Guru (Rule One Context)

A Guru:

-   Manages a professional investment fund
-   Files quarterly Form 13F reports with the SEC
-   Has a public track record of long-term investing
-   Is widely recognized within the investment community
-   Often has a defined investment philosophy or strategy

When analyzing Guru ownership in research:

-   Check historical and current 13F filings
-   Identify position sizing trends
-   Evaluate whether Gurus are accumulating or exiting
-   Use ownership as context --- not as a standalone buy signal

## Interpretation Guidance for Claude

When a Rule One document asks:

-   "Are any Gurus invested?"
-   "Has a Guru recently sold?"
-   "List Gurus owning the company."

Claude should:

1.  Reference only the above list.
2.  Confirm ownership via SEC 13F filings when applicable.
3.  Distinguish between:
    -   Current ownership
    -   Recent exits
    -   Significant position sizing (\>5% portfolio weight if available)
4.  Treat Guru activity as contextual validation --- not proof of
    investment merit.

## Structural Principle

Guru ownership is:

-   A signal of professional interest
-   A starting point for deeper research
-   Never a substitute for independent valuation and conviction
