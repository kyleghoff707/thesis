# Story Form II

## Technical Summary

Purpose: Complete the conviction framework by validating valuation,
stress-testing risks, and defining execution strategy.

Covers Sections 5--8: 5. Valuation Confirmation 6. Inversion and
Rebuttal Analysis 7. Initial Trading Strategy 8. Investment Strategy

Objective: Ensure growth assumptions are rational, risks are rebutted,
and capital deployment has a predefined plan.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 5. Valuation Confirmation

Before acting on valuation models (MOS, PBT, 10 Cap), confirm:

## Growth Quality Checks

-   Is growth fueled by debt?
-   Is FCF/debt within acceptable thresholds?
-   Is EPS/debt stable?
-   Is growth organic or acquisition-driven?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Debt-Fueled Growth Test

If revenue growth rises alongside debt:

Evaluate: - FCF / Debt ratio - EPS / Debt ratio - Debt relative to
earnings capacity

Debt-fueled growth must remain sustainable.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Organic vs Acquisition Growth

Questions:

-   Does the company frequently acquire businesses?
-   Are acquisitions small relative to market cap?
-   Are acquisitions within circle of competence?
-   Is acquisition track record successful?

Acquisition Red Flags: - Large transformational mergers - Culture
mismatch risk - Debt-financed expansion - Overpaying for growth

Organic growth preferred unless acquisition competency is proven.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 6. Inversion and Rebuttal Analysis

Purpose: Stress test thesis by asking how the business could fail.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Risk Identification

-   What risks does management disclose?
-   What historical failures apply?
-   What macro or competitive threats exist?
-   Are partnerships introducing new risk?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Growth Ceiling Analysis

Definition: A point where future growth becomes structurally limited.

Test FGR rationality:

1.  Project revenue 10 years forward using FGR.
2.  Apply Rule of 72 to estimate doubling frequency.
3.  Compare future market share against total industry size.

If projected market share expansion is unrealistic → reduce FGR.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Structured Inversion Framework

For each key thesis reason:

Thesis: "Company has dominant moat."

Inversion: "Competitor will erode moat."

Rebuttal: - Market positioning differences - Broader product mix -
Distribution advantages - Downturn resilience

Every inversion requires evidence-based rebuttal.

If rebuttal is weak → reduce position size or avoid investment.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 7. Initial Trading Strategy

Purpose: Determine tactical entry method.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Valuation During Events

Backtest prior dislocations: - Which valuation method performed best? -
Was 10 Cap most accurate? - Did MOS overshoot? - Did PBT provide safety?

Choose valuation lens accordingly.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Growth Stage Classification

Identify stage:

-   Early Growth
-   Rapid Growth
-   Slowing Growth
-   Early Maturity
-   Late Maturity
-   Decline

Growth stage affects: - FGR assumptions - Position size - Holding period
expectations

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Tactical Tools

-   Price alerts
-   Staggered tranches
-   STO ROP for entry
-   Avoid emotional buying

Entry plan must be written before capital is deployed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 8. Investment Strategy

Purpose: Define long-term capital plan.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Exit Discipline

If story remains intact: - Exit around 120% of intrinsic value - Sell
ITM ROCs for controlled exit - Or hold indefinitely if thesis unchanged

If story changes: Have predefined plan.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## PACE Framework

Primary: Story changed and above intrinsic value → sell ROCs to exit.

Alternate: Above intrinsic value + macro weakness → reduce exposure.

Contingency: Insiders selling aggressively → reduce or exit.

Emergency: Thesis fundamentally broken → STC immediately.

Plan must be written while unemotional.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Structural Insight

Story Form II ensures:

-   Growth assumptions are rational.
-   Valuation is validated.
-   Risks are inverted and rebutted.
-   Entry strategy is predefined.
-   Exit plan is unemotional and structured.

Conviction without a plan is speculation. Conviction with structure is
disciplined investing.
