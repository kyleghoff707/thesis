# Story Form I

## Technical Summary

Purpose: Transform research (One-Pager + Pitch Deck) into a coherent
narrative that stress-tests conviction before capital deployment.

Covers Sections 1--4: 1. Event Analysis 2. Meaning Analysis 3. Moat
Analysis 4. Management Analysis

Objective: Move from surface-level screening to deep qualitative
conviction.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. Event Analysis

Key Question: Is the company currently experiencing an "event" that
caused price dislocation?

## Event Framework

Evaluate:

-   What caused the price drop?
-   Has this happened before?
-   How long did recovery take?
-   Is this situation similar or different?
-   Did prior recovery require debt?
-   What are analysts currently saying?

Goal: Determine whether the event is temporary (opportunity) or
structural (thesis risk).

Conclusion should state: - Nature of event - Historical recovery
comparison - Probability of resolution

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Meaning Analysis

Purpose: Deepen understanding of business drivers beyond financial
statements.

## Radar Transfer

Most Radar metrics transfer directly from previous documents.

## KPI Analysis

KPIs vary by: - Sector - Industry - Individual company model

Process:

1.  Identify industry KPIs.
2.  Identify company-specific KPIs.
3.  Compare against competitors.
4.  Evaluate trend consistency.

Examples of KPI categories: - Same-store sales - Customer growth -
Revenue per unit - Margin stability - Inventory turnover

Goal: Determine if operational metrics confirm financial strength.

Conclusion should state: - Are KPIs improving? - Is competitive position
strengthening or weakening?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3. Moat Analysis

Purpose: Assess competitive durability.

## Barrier to Entry Questions

-   Does the company identify barriers?
-   Could a competitor enter easily?
-   If you had capital equal to market cap, could you compete?

Common barriers: - Regulation - Shelf space - Capital intensity - Brand
recognition - Network effects

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Pricing Power

Can the company raise prices during inflation or cost increases?

Indicators: - Margin stability - Historical inflation performance -
Customer loyalty evidence

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Field Research Component

Qualitative inputs: - Customer experience - Supplier relationships -
Brand perception - Competitive comparison

Goal: Confirm moat durability through both data and real-world
observation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4. Management Analysis

Purpose: Determine talent + integrity.

## CEO Compensation Review

-   Review Proxy Statement
-   Compare pay to peers
-   Evaluate pay-performance alignment

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Buyback Assessment

Questions:

-   Is company conducting buybacks?
-   Are shares repurchased below intrinsic value?
-   Is buyback size rational relative to cash flow?

Buybacks above intrinsic value = capital misallocation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Capital Efficiency Metrics

Evaluate:

-   Maintenance CapEx as % of Cash from Operations
-   Free Cash Flow as % of Earnings (≥75% preferred)
-   Owner Earnings consistency

Low maintenance CapEx + strong FCF conversion = durable model.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Management Conclusion

Summarize:

-   Capital allocation skill
-   Integrity signals
-   Operational discipline
-   Shareholder alignment

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Structural Insight

Story Form I transforms research into conviction by answering:

-   Is the event temporary?
-   Do KPIs confirm strength?
-   Is the moat durable?
-   Is management trustworthy?

Only after these four sections pass should valuation and strategy
proceed.
