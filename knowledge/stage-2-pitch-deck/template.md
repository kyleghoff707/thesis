Rule #1 Pitch Deck Template

Technical Summary

Purpose:
Provide a structured 10-part research framework for evaluating a company before capital deployment.

The Pitch Deck moves from:

Context → Understanding → Competitive Strength → Financial Strength → Risk → Valuation → Conclusion

It is both:
	•	A research checklist
	•	A conviction document

⸻

1. Radar

Objective:

Establish context.

Questions:
	1.	How did you become aware of the company?
	•	Gurus
	•	News
	•	Three Circles
	•	Search
	2.	Which Gurus are currently invested?
	3.	Which Gurus added >4% at current or higher price?
	4.	Is there a current Event?
	5.	What specifically caused the business to go on sale?

Radar identifies:
Opportunity vs noise.

⸻

2. Simple & Predictable

Objective:

Confirm clarity and business durability.

Questions:
	•	In simple terms, how does the company make money?
	•	Is the industry understandable?
	•	Is it cyclical? If so, describe the cycle.
	•	What specific problem does the company solve?

Insert:
Price chart for visual context.

Pass criteria:
You can explain it clearly and predict its long-term direction.

⸻

3. Dominant Market Position

Objective:

Determine competitive strength.

Questions:
	•	Briefly describe company dominance.
	•	What is the Rule One Moat Score?
	•	What is the company’s niche?
	•	How did they become dominant?
	•	Who are the key competitors?
	•	How do they compare?

Dominance = historical superiority + defensible position.

⸻

4. Large Barrier to Entry & Moats

Objective:

Assess durability of advantage.

Questions:
	•	What barriers prevent entry?
	•	What type of moat exists?
	•	Has the moat strengthened or weakened?
	•	Why can’t competitors overtake them?
	•	Is the moat anti-fragile?

Moat must be:
Structural, not temporary.

⸻

5. Free Cash Flow Generative

Objective:

Confirm economic engine strength.

Metrics:
	•	10-year FCF Ratio (FCF / Earnings)
	•	FCF Growth
	•	Consistency of conversion

Insert:
Growth Metrics Chart
FCF Growth & Ratio Chart

Strong FCF supports valuation confidence.

⸻

6. Management Talent & Integrity

Objective:

Evaluate leadership quality.

Questions:
	•	CEO background & track record
	•	Capital allocation skill (ROIC / ROE / ROA)
	•	Integrity signals
	•	Insider ownership
	•	Insider buying/selling
	•	External reputation (Glassdoor, Comparably)

Management must:
Create value AND protect shareholders.

⸻

7. ROE / ROIC / ROA & Debt

Objective:

Analyze return quality and leverage.

Questions:
	•	10-year return trends
	•	Are returns inflated by debt?
	•	Debt trend over time

Insert:
Debt trend visual

High returns + low leverage = strongest profile.

⸻

8. Strong Balance Sheet

Objective:

Assess financial resilience.

Questions:
	•	Has balance sheet improved under current management?
	•	What is the Current Ratio?
	•	Liquidity strength?

Insert:
Balance Sheet visual

Survivability matters.

⸻

9. Limited Exposure to P.E.S.T Risks

Objective:

Stress test macro vulnerability.

Evaluate:

Political
Economic
Social
Technological

Key Question:

What could make this company go off the rails?

Every risk must have:
Evidence-based rebuttal.

⸻

10. Valuation

Objective:

Determine rational buy prices.

Questions:
	•	Why will the industry be strong in 10 years?
	•	Why will this company be strong in 10 years?
	•	What valuation adjustments were made?
	•	What are the three buy prices?

⸻

MOS Inputs:
	•	Growth Rate
	•	EPS (TTM or adjusted?)
	•	Future P/E (≤ 2× Growth, not above historical high)
	•	MARR = 15%
	•	Sticker Price
	•	MOS Price

⸻

PBT Inputs:
	•	FCF Ratio
	•	FCF per share
	•	Years to payback

⸻

Ten Cap Inputs:
	•	Cash from Operations
	•	CapEx
	•	Maintenance CapEx %
	•	Tax Provision
	•	Owner Earnings
	•	Shares Outstanding
	•	OE per share

⸻

Conclusion Section

Summarize:
	•	Understandable?
	•	Moat strength?
	•	Growth quality?
	•	Management quality?
	•	Valuation attractiveness?
	•	Anti-fragile?
	•	Add to Watchlist?

This is the final conviction checkpoint.

⸻

Structural Insight

The Pitch Deck ensures:

No blind spots
No skipped analysis
No emotional investing

It forces structured conviction before capital deployment.
