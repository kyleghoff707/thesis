# Pitch Deck II

## Technical Summary

Focus Areas: 4. Large Barrier to Entry 5. Free Cash Flow Generative 6.
Management Integrity & Capital Allocation

Goal: Identify durable competitive advantages and trustworthy
leadership.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. Large Barrier to Entry

Barrier: Prevents competitors from entering a niche.

Moat: Protects company from competitors already inside the niche.

Layered Defense: Barriers + Moats = Competitive durability.

Common Barriers: - Capital requirements - Regulatory hurdles - Shelf
space limitations

Common Moats: - Brand - Network - Switching costs - Price advantage -
Secrets/Patents - Toll bridge

We want wide, durable moats.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Evaluating the Moat

Questions:

-   Has the moat changed over time?
-   Are competitors copying the model?
-   Is the company innovating to stay ahead?
-   Would it be stronger in 20 years?
-   Has it survived Black Swan events?

Anti-fragility preferred.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3. Free Cash Flow (FCF)

EPS ≠ Cash

Free Cash Flow: Operating Cash Flow -- CapEx

CapEx: - Maintenance - Growth

Key Questions:

-   Is FCF positive?
-   Is it growing?
-   What is FCF / Net Income ratio?

Rule One FCF Ratio benchmark: \~1.0 or higher is healthy.

Uses of FCF: - Growth - Dividends - Buybacks

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4. Management Evaluation

CEO Focus:

-   Track record
-   Capital allocation skill
-   Integrity
-   Insider ownership
-   Reputation

Return Metrics:

ROE = Net Income / Equity\
ROIC = Net Income / (Equity + LT Debt)\
ROA = Net Income / Total Assets

Strong management: - Consistent high returns - Transparent
communication - Acknowledges mistakes - Aligns incentives with
shareholders

Integrity is qualitative but critical.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 5. Capital Allocation Skill

CEO deploys capital in:

-   Growth initiatives
-   Buybacks
-   Dividends

Look for improving return metrics during tenure.

Insider behavior: - Are insiders buying? - Excessive selling may be red
flag.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Structural Insight

Durable Moat + Positive FCF + Honest Management\
= High probability of long-term compounding.
