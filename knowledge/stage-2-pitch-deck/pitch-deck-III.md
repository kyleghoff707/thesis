# Pitch Deck III

## Technical Summary

Focus Areas: 7. High ROE/ROIC/ROA 8. Strong Balance Sheet 9. Limited
Exposure to P.E.S.T. Risk

Objective: Ensure returns are real, debt is controlled, and macro risks
are survivable.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. ROE / ROIC / ROA

ROE = Net Income / Equity\
ROIC = Net Income / (Equity + LT Debt)\
ROA = Net Income / Average Total Assets

Key Questions:

-   Are returns consistent over 10 years?
-   Are returns improving?
-   Is debt artificially inflating ROE?

High returns with low debt = strongest profile.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Debt Analysis

No debt is ideal.

Red Flag: Debt \> 3 years of earnings or FCF.

Debt reduces ROIC and ROA.

During recessions: Debt becomes dangerous.

Rule One Debt Score can decline if: 1. Debt increases 2. Earnings
decrease

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3. Strong Balance Sheet

Balance Sheet = Assets -- Liabilities = Equity

Look For:

-   Positive equity
-   Growing equity trend
-   Healthy current ratio

Current Ratio = Current Assets / Current Liabilities

\~2:1 ideal\
\<1 problematic

Strong balance sheets survive downturns.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4. P.E.S.T Framework

Used for inversion (risk assessment).

Political: Regulations, tariffs, compliance changes

Economic: Recessions, competition, macro cycles

Social: Consumer perception shifts

Technological: Disruption risk, platform replacement

Each risk must include rebuttal.

If no rebuttal exists → thesis weakened.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 5. Rebuttal Logic

When identifying risks:

1.  Define the risk clearly
2.  Use historical data
3.  Provide rational counterargument

We are stress-testing the "Wonderful Company" thesis.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
