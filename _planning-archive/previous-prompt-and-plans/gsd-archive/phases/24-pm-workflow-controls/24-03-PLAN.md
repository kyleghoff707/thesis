<objective>
Build the checkpoint review UI: the CheckpointPanel container that appears during mid-pipeline pauses, the DataGapsPanel for surfacing what agents couldn't access, the CheckpointCommentBox for per-section feedback with file attachments, and the SectionRenderer comment icon integration.

Purpose: This is the PM's primary control surface during pipeline execution (per D-01). Instead of passively waiting for generation to complete, the PM reviews each wave's output, provides feedback, fills data gaps, and steers the analysis. This is the hedge fund model -- the PM doesn't just read the final output, they direct the research at every checkpoint.
Output: 3 new components + 1 modified component.
</objective>
