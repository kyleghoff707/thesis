What varies

A — Bold 700, medium round dot, normal gap. The baseline.

B — Bold 700, small dot, tight gap. More subtle — dot is a hint, not a feature.

C — Bold 700, large dot. Reads immediately as "i". Most prominent.

D — Extra-bold 800, medium dot. Heavier "1" pops more against medium-weight text.

E — Medium 500, medium dot. Same weight as surrounding text — only color differentiates.

F — SemiBold 600, medium dot. Gentle weight bump — in between E and A.

G — Bold 700, medium dot, wide gap. Dot floats higher — more air between dot and numeral.

H — Bold 700, square dot (rounded corners). More geometric/structured.
