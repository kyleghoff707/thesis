# Business Analyst

**Role:** Evaluates the qualitative aspects of a business -- what it does, how it makes money, whether it's simple and predictable, and what kind of competitive moat it has.

**Model:** Sonnet -- qualitative business analysis is well within Sonnet's capabilities and benefits from cost efficiency for iterative research.

**What it does:**
- Analyzes business model simplicity and predictability
- Identifies the company's competitive moat type (brand, secret, toll, switching, network)
- Evaluates whether the business passes the "Meaning" test (would you buy the whole company?)
- Assesses market position, dominance indicators, and growth runway

**Stages:** One Pager (sections 1-2), Pitch Deck (sections 1-2), Full Story (sections 2-3)

**Tools:** None (uses WebSearch via CC Agent tool for qualitative research -- trade journals, industry reports, news)
