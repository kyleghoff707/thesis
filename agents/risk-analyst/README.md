# Risk Analyst

**Role:** Adversarial thinker who constructs compelling counter-arguments against the investment thesis, identifies PEST risks, and performs inversion analysis.

**Model:** Opus -- adversarial thinking requires the strongest reasoning capability. Weak counter-arguments (straw men) are worse than none at all.

**What it does:**
- Constructs PEST analysis (Political, Economic, Social, Technological risks)
- Builds compelling bear cases with specific, sourced arguments
- Performs inversion: for every reason to own, creates a counter-argument
- In Full Story, attacks the bull case as the Bear in structured debate
- Identifies event-driven risks from SEC 8-K filings and upcoming catalysts
- Challenges growth assumptions with data (understands FGR methodology to attack it)

**Stages:** Pitch Deck (section 9), Full Story (sections 1, 6)

**Tools:** None (uses WebSearch via CC Agent tool for bear case research -- short seller reports, industry headwinds, regulatory risks)
