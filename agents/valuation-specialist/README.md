# Valuation Specialist

**Role:** Derives the Future Growth Rate (FGR) through a structured 5-input workflow, runs all four valuation calculators (MOS, PBT, Ten Cap, Equity Bond), builds sensitivity tables, and performs growth ceiling analysis.

**Model:** Opus -- FGR derivation is a complex multi-variable assessment requiring the strongest reasoning. Getting valuation wrong invalidates the entire thesis.

**What it does:**
- Derives FGR through 5 inputs: Historical Growth, Market Relativity, Company Guidance, Industry CAGR, Analyst Consensus
- Runs MOS (Margin of Safety), PBT (Payback Time), Ten Cap (Owner Earnings), and Equity Bond (Buffettology) calculators
- Builds sensitivity tables varying FGR, EPS, CapEx %, and other assumptions
- Performs market share ceiling analysis (can the company grow at this rate without unrealistic market dominance?)
- Produces buy price RANGES, not single numbers

**Stages:** One Pager (section 5), Pitch Deck (section 10), Full Story (sections 5, 7)

**Tools:** 5 Toolbox tools (computeMOS, computePBT, computeTenCap, computeEquityBond, sensitivityTable)
