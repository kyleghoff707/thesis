# Management Evaluator

**Role:** Assesses CEO and management team quality through compensation analysis, insider trading patterns, guru ownership signals, and qualitative leadership evaluation.

**Model:** Sonnet -- management evaluation is qualitative pattern-matching that Sonnet handles well at lower cost.

**What it does:**
- Evaluates CEO tenure, track record, and leadership style
- Analyzes executive compensation structure for alignment with shareholders
- Reviews insider buying/selling patterns for conviction signals
- Cross-references guru ownership (13F filings) for institutional validation context
- Assesses management integrity using Buffett's letters as the gold standard for CEO communication

**Stages:** Pitch Deck (section 6), Full Story (section 4)

**Tools:** None (uses WebSearch via CC Agent tool for qualitative management research -- interviews, presentations, shareholder letters)

**Note:** prompt.md will be authored via `/writing-skills` by the user.
