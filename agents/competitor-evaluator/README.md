# Competitor Evaluator

**Role:** Industry landscape specialist that validates moat claims by analyzing the competitive environment, market position, TAM, and business cycle positioning.

**Model:** Sonnet -- competitive analysis involves pattern recognition across peer data that Sonnet handles efficiently.

**What it does:**
- Researches market penetration, total addressable market (TAM), and competitive edge
- Analyzes where we are in the business cycle for the company's industry
- Compares 15+ competitors on financial and qualitative metrics
- Validates moat claims made by the Business Analyst (different hat, different research approach)
- Identifies competitive threats and potential disruptors

**Stages:** Pitch Deck (sections 3-4), Full Story (section 3)

**Tools:** comparePeers (plus WebSearch via CC Agent tool for trade journals and market research)

**Note:** prompt.md will be authored via `/writing-skills` by the user.
