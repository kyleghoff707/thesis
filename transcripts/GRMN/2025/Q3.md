We are pleased with the performance of the Outdoor segment, but delivering back-to-back years of double-digit revenue growth has been more challenging than originally anticipated, following the 1-year anniversary of the highly successful product launches in this segment, most notably the fenix 8. Turning to the Marine segment, revenue increased 20% to $267 million, with growth across multiple categories, including chartplotters, audio and cartography.
