    Section-specific checks (derived directly from curriculum):

    **Radar (company_info / minimum_standards):**
    - [critical] Event analysis: narrative mentions event, price drop, catalyst, or dislocation
    - [critical] 3 Ms mentioned: Meaning, Moat, Management referenced in narrative
    - [supplementary] Company snapshot with key metrics in data field

    **Simple & Predictable (company_info / minimum_standards):**
    - [critical] Business model clarity: narrative explains how company makes money (revenue sources)
    - [critical] Predictability assessment: references to revenue trend, earnings consistency, or cash flow stability
    - [supplementary] Cyclicality addressed

    **Market Position (market_position):**
    - [critical] Market share data: narrative references market share percentage or ranking
    - [critical] Competitor comparison: mentions competitors by name
    - [supplementary] TAM (Total Addressable Market) referenced

    **Barriers & Moats (barriers_and_moats):**
    - [critical] Specific moat type identified: regex for brand|switching|toll bridge|price advantage|secret|patent|network
    - [critical] Moat durability assessment: references to 10-20 year durability, anti-fragility, or enduring problem
    - [supplementary] Multiple moat types identified
    - [supplementary] Competitor copying/threat assessment

    **FCF (growth_metrics for section 5):**
    - [critical] FCF calculation present: narrative mentions FCF, free cash flow, or operating cash flow minus capex
    - [critical] FCF ratio: narrative mentions FCF ratio, FCF/net income, or FCF/earnings
    - [supplementary] Maintenance vs growth capex distinction
    - [supplementary] Shareholder benefit discussion (buybacks, dividends, reinvestment)

    **Management (management):**
    - [critical] CEO evaluation: narrative mentions CEO by name or discusses CEO track record
    - [critical] Insider ownership: references insider buying/selling, ownership stake, or skin in game
    - [supplementary] Capital allocation assessment
    - [supplementary] Integrity assessment (promises vs follow-through)
    - [supplementary] B.A.G. (Big Audacious Goal) or strategic vision

    **ROE/ROIC/Debt (growth_metrics for section 7):**
    - [critical] Return metrics present: narrative mentions ROE, ROIC, or ROA with values
    - [critical] Debt analysis: narrative mentions debt, leverage, or debt-to-earnings
    - [supplementary] ROE vs ROIC comparison (debt distortion check)
    - [supplementary] 10-year consistency assessment

    **Balance Sheet (balance_sheet):**
    - [critical] Current ratio or liquidity assessment
    - [critical] Equity trend discussed (positive/growing)
    - [supplementary] Assets vs liabilities breakdown
    - [supplementary] Balance sheet strength across downturns

    **PEST (pest_risks):**
    - [critical] All 4 PEST categories covered: check narrative for Political AND Economic AND Social AND Technological
    - [critical] Rebuttal present: narrative contains rebuttal, counter-argument, or evidence-backed response
    - [supplementary] Historical resilience evidence

    **Valuation (valuation_summary):**
    - [critical] All 4 methods present: MOS AND PBT AND (Ten Cap OR 10 Cap OR Owner Earnings) AND Equity Bond
    - [critical] FGR derivation: narrative mentions FGR or future growth rate with at least 2 of the 5 inputs (historical, market, guidance, sector, analyst)
    - [critical] Buy price or price target present: narrative mentions buy price, sticker price, or margin of safety price
    - [supplementary] Sensitivity analysis or range of values
    - [supplementary] 10-year outlook assessment

    **Exempt sections (overall_verdict, synthesis, PSR sections):** Return { score: 100, checks: [], passed: true }.

    **Scoring formula per D-04:**
    - Each check has weight: critical = 2, supplementary = 1
    - Score = (sum of passed weights / sum of total weights) * 100, rounded
    - passed = true if score >= 50 (at least half of critical elements present)
