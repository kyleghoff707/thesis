<objective>
Make filing section extraction form-aware (10-K vs 10-Q item numbers) and expand pre-processing depth to 5 years of 10-Ks and 8 quarters of 10-Qs.

Purpose: 10-Q extraction currently fails because SECTION_MAP uses 10-K item numbers. 10-Q MD&A is Item 2 (not 7), Financial Statements is Item 1 (not 8). This breaks the quarterly-reader PSR agent. Expanding depth covers a full business cycle for cross-validation (per D-06).

Output: Form-aware filingSections.js with separate SECTION_MAPs, updated preprocess-filings.js with increased depth and collision-safe filenames, and comprehensive tests.
</objective>
